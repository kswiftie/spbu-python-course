# Available Bets in Roulette

Here's a breakdown of the bets you can place in our roulette game:

### 1. Red/Black

• Description: Bet on whether the ball will land on a red or black slot.

• Payout: 1:1 (You win your bet amount + your original bet back).

• Example: You bet 10 units on red. The ball lands on red. You win 10 units and get your original 10 units back.

### 2. Even/Odd

• Description: Bet on whether the ball will land on an even or odd number.

• Payout: 1:1.

• Example: You bet 50 units on even. The ball lands on 16. You win 50 units and get your original 50 units back.

### 3. 1-18/19-36 (Low/High)

• Description: Bet on whether the ball will land on a number between 1 and 18 (low) or between 19 and 36 (high).

• Payout: 1:1.

• Example: You bet 20 units on high. The ball lands on 25. You win 20 units and get your original 20 units back.

### 4. Dozens

• Description: Bet on one of three columns (1-12, 13-24, 25-36) or one of three dozens (1-12, 13-24, 25-36).

• Payout: 2:1.

• Example: You bet 30 units on the second dozen (13-24). The ball lands on 19. You win 60 units and get your original 30
units back.

### 5. Sixline (6 Numbers)

• Description: Bet on six consecutive numbers.

• Payout: 5:1.

• Example: You bet 40 units on 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6. The ball lands on 3. You win 200 units and get your original 40 units
back.

### 6. Street (3 Numbers)

• Description: Bet on three consecutive numbers.

• Payout: 11:1.

• Example: You bet 10 units on 10, 11, 12. The ball lands on 11. You win 110 units and get your original 10 units back.

### 7. Split (2 Numbers)

• Description: Bet on two adjacent numbers.

• Payout: 17:1.

• Example: You bet 10 units on 7 and 8. The ball lands on 8. You win 170 units and get your original 10 units back.

### 8. Straight Up (1 Number)

• Description: Bet on a single specific number.

• Payout: 35:1.

• Example: You bet 5 units on 23. The ball lands on 23. You win 175 units and get your original 5 units back.
